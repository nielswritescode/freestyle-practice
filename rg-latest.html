Guidelines
  1. Pick a beat (using the music player or use your own)
  2. Rhyme!
    • Level 1: Simply say the words on count 4. x x x Bed x x x Shed
    • Level 2: Make small mundane sentences with the words. x x I lie in bed. x x I'm in my shed.
    • Level 3: Weave the sentences into a (somewhat) coherent context. I don't want to get up, I just lie in bed. I wish I wasn't sleeping in this awful shed.
  3. The main purpose is to enjoy! Make mistakes, go offbeat and make up nonsense as you go.
Practices
  1. Say the word on count 4. On count 8, come up with your own rhyme.
    • Level 2: Think of 2 words and choose the second one.
  2. If you can't think of any, say a nonsense rhyme and go back to it after the practice has ended.
