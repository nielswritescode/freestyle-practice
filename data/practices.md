Practices for RhymeFlow.

How this file works:
- Each "# Heading" below starts a new practice. Its heading text becomes the
  button label shown under "Practices" in the app.
- Everything under a heading, until the next "# Heading" (or the end of the
  file), becomes that practice's instructions.
- Numbered lines (1. 2. 3. ...) become the numbered steps.
- An indented line starting with "-" becomes a sub-point attached to the
  step above it — use "**Label:**" at the start of it for a bold label like
  "Level 2:" (see the example below).
- Add a brand new practice by just adding another "# Heading" section
  anywhere below — no other changes needed, the app picks it up automatically.
- This paragraph and everything above the first "# Heading" is just notes
  for you — it's never shown in the app.

# Simple Rhyme

1. Say the word shown on count 4. On count 8, come up with your own rhyme.
   - **Level 2:** Think of 2 words and choose the second one.
2. If you can't think of any, say a nonsense rhyme and go back to it after the practice has ended.

# Single Word

1. Practice level 1, 2 or 3 with single words. 

  - **Level 2:** Invert. Say your own rhyme first.

2. Investigate where you get stuck and focus on one specific area at a time.
